About Minakshi Multispeciality Dental Clinic

"मीनाक्षी दातांचा दवाखाना" — We Cares Your Smile

At Minakshi Multispeciality Dental Clinic & Implant Centre, we are committed to delivering high-quality, comprehensive oral healthcare in a patient-friendly, hygienic environment. Located on Saswad Road (near Purandar Medical and Angraj Dhaba) in Kondhwa / Yewalewadi, Pune, our clinic provides both routine dental hygiene care and specialized surgical dental procedures with the highest clinical standards.

Strict Sterilization Protocol

Patient safety is paramount. We follow rigorous hospital-grade autoclaving and instrument sterilization protocol in an ISO 9001:2015 certified environment.

Gentle & Painless Procedures

From single-sitting root canals to gentle tooth extractions, we use modern techniques to ensure a comfortable, anxiety-free dental experience.

Affordable & Honest Care

Transparent consultations with zero hidden fees. Fair pricing designed to make multispeciality dental health accessible for every family.
